01 · Primary Responsibilities & SOP OPERATIONAL DUTIES

  • DUTY 01
    Rapid Video Editing & Packaging

    Turn raw video and audio into finished high-retention content. Cut dead air, synchronize sound effects, grade color, and deliver compelling visual rhythm.

  • DUTY 02
    Strict Turnaround Time Adherence

    Execute edits within established turnaround windows (under 24 hours for short-form clips, under 48 hours for long-form assets). Flag delays before deadlines, not after.

  • DUTY 03
    Visual Asset & Graphic Design

    Design clean thumbnails, carousel slides, infographics, and motion graphics that support organic publishing campaigns without generic templates or visual clutter.

  • DUTY 04
    Asset Library Organization

    Maintain an indexed repository of project files, B-roll footage, sound effects, client brand assets, and reusable design elements for instant retrieval.

  1. 08:30
