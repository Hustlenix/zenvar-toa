    Discovery FrameworkSELL OUTCOMES, NOT FEATURES

    Instead of "we build websites with five pages," prefer: "we help your business create a professional online presence that makes it easier for potential customers to understand your services and contact you."

    Before pitching, understand: what the business does, who its customers are, how customers currently find it, whether the website generates leads, what repetitive processes consume time, what communication bottlenecks exist, and what business outcome the client wants.
