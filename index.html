Zenvar is a real business, not a project. We are paid to deliver results for clients in three areas: content growth, website builds, and business automation. This agreement defines how we work, how we are measured, and what is expected of every member.

READ FULLY BEFORE SIGNING

01

Scope of supply

Three service lines. If work does not serve one of them, it does not ship.

  1. LINE AContent growth
  2. LINE BWebsite builds
  3. LINE CBusiness automation
02

Operating checks

EVERY DECISION AND EVERY TASK PASSES THREE QUESTIONS

Q1

SIGNAL OVER NOISE

Spend time on work that moves a client result or a team metric forward. Planning, tweaking, and looking busy are not signal.

Q2

ATTACK THE BOTTLENECK

Before starting new work, identify what is actually limiting progress, for you or for the team, and fix that first.

Q3

NUMBERS OVER FEELINGS

“I think I improved” is not an update. Bring the number: leads sent, revenue, deliverables shipped, deadlines hit.

04

Procedure

WORK STANDARDS · APPLY DAILY · FOLLOW IN ORDER

  1. STEP 01

    Show up daily with intent. No excuses for avoidable misses; if you miss work, you fill the gap later.

  2. STEP 02

    Complete assigned work on time. Flag the delay before the deadline, not after.

  3. STEP 03

    Apply judgment before escalating. Check the relevant SOP or checklist first. If it answers your question, don't send it to Hemanathan for approval.

  4. STEP 04

    Take ownership of your role's outcome, not just your task list.

  5. STEP 05

    Give and accept direct feedback, on your own work and on teammates'.

  6. STEP 06

    Improve at least one relevant skill every week.

05

Inspection

ACCOUNTABILITY · RUNS WEEKLY · PER ROLE

  • GAUGE i

    Work completed against what was assigned

  • GAUGE ii

    The weekly metric for that role (parts list, item by item)

  • GAUGE iii

    One blocker or bottleneck that slowed the work down

06

We solve problems together and communicate honestly.

We do not tolerate blame-shifting, ego, or repeated unexplained misses.

Effort is respected. Consistency is expected. Improvement is required. Results are how we measure it.

07

Approvals

  • §2 Follow the standards in Sections 04 and 05
  • §3 Contribute to the team's results, not just your own task list
